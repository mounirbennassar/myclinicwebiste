"Breaking Barriers in Healthcare" - innovation from My Clinic's first annual scientific conference.

A mock editorial detail page for the latest My Clinic announcement, designed with a cleaner reading experience, stronger hierarchy, and practical calls to action.

8 min readDr. Layla Al-RashedScientific Affairs

My Clinic hosted its first annual scientific conference under the theme "Breaking Barriers in Healthcare", bringing together clinicians, researchers, and care leaders to discuss practical innovation across outpatient medicine.

The event focused on measurable improvements in access, safety, patient communication, and coordinated care. Sessions highlighted how multidisciplinary teams can use technology and shared clinical pathways to reduce friction for patients and support earlier intervention.

Designed around the patient journey

Conference tracks covered chronic disease management, women's health, pediatric care, telemedicine, home healthcare, and diagnostic workflows. Each session was framed around one core question: how can care become easier to understand, easier to access, and more consistent across branches?

Speakers shared examples from My Clinic teams, including remote follow-up workflows, faster referral loops between specialties, and patient education tools that help families make confident decisions.

"Innovation is only meaningful when patients feel the difference in their appointment, their recovery, and their everyday life."

Dr. Layla Al-Rashed · Scientific Affairs

What comes next

Following the conference, My Clinic will continue developing practical programs that connect specialists, nurses, digital services, and home care teams around the same patient goals.

The next phase includes expanded clinical education sessions, broader digital follow-up options, and deeper collaboration between branch teams to keep patient care consistent from first visit to follow-up.
