What we treat online

Right for a great
many things.

Telemedicine handles a wide range of everyday and ongoing care. If a condition needs hands-on examination, your doctor will arrange the right in-person or home visit.

Cold, flu & fever

Coughs, sore throats, sinus and seasonal infections.

Skin conditions

Rashes, acne, eczema and allergic reactions, reviewed by photo.

Mental health

Anxiety, stress, low mood and sleep — with licensed therapists.

Chronic care

Diabetes, blood-pressure and thyroid follow-ups and monitoring.

Prescription refills

Renew regular medications without a clinic trip.

Children's health

Pediatric advice for fevers, rashes and common childhood illness.

Digestive issues

Stomach upset, reflux, nausea and general GI concerns.

Lab & result review

Order tests and walk through results with your doctor.

Not for emergencies. For chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding or any life-threatening symptom, call Saudi Red Crescent 997 or go to the nearest ER.

For general consultations, our average wait is under twelve minutes, day or night. Specialist video appointments are usually scheduled within the same day. Members on Plus and Family plans get a priority queue.
Yes. When clinically appropriate, your doctor issues a digitally signed e-prescription routed to the pharmacy of your choice, along with sick notes and chronic-medication refills. Certain controlled substances cannot be prescribed remotely under Saudi regulations — your doctor will advise.
Most major insurers — Tawuniya, Bupa Arabia, MedGulf, Allianz and AXA — now reimburse virtual consultations. We'll verify your eligibility before the call, and issue an itemised digital invoice you can submit if you pay upfront.
Anything requiring hands-on examination, imaging, or in-person procedures. Telemedicine is not for emergencies — for chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding or other urgent symptoms, call 997 or go to the nearest ER. When needed, your doctor will refer you to one of our clinics or a home visit.
Every consultation is end-to-end encrypted and never recorded. Visit notes, prescriptions and results are stored on PDPL-compliant infrastructure in your patient portal, and you control who in your family can access them.
No download is required — consults run securely in your browser from any phone, tablet or computer with a camera. The My Clinic app is available for members who want appointment reminders, refill tracking and their full record in one place.
