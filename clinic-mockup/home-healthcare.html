Our home-care teams operate from our Jeddah (4 branches) and Riyadh (1 branch) facilities, with full coverage across the municipal city limits of both cities. Outer suburbs can be coordinated with advance review — call our coordinators to confirm your address.
Coverage varies by policy. We're in-network with most major insurers (Tawuniya, Bupa Arabia, MedGulf, Allianz, AXA). Many home nursing, physician, and physiotherapy visits are covered under premium and post-surgical recovery policies. Our billing team verifies pre-authorization with your provider before any visit.
For non-urgent visits, our average response is under four hours. Same-day visits are typically available when requested before 2 PM. Specialty appointments (orthopedic consultants, physiotherapy plans) are usually scheduled 24–48 hours in advance.
No. Home care is structured for scheduled, non-emergency clinical visits, chronic-disease follow-ups, and rehabilitation. For acute emergencies, call Saudi Red Crescent (997) or proceed to the nearest ER. Our coordinators can advise on the right path if you're unsure.
