Mobile Care Capabilities

Our Comprehensive Home Services

Physician Home Visits

Schedule general medicine doctors and specialty consultants to visit your home for clinical consults, physical checkups, prescription renewals, and care guidance.

Skilled Nursing Care

Licensed nurses providing standard home nursing including wound dressing, vital signs monitoring, injections, intravenous (IV) therapy, and post-operative recovery support.

Home Physiotherapy

Customized home rehabilitation sessions directed by certified physiotherapists. Ideal for orthopedic recovery, neurological rehab, elderly mobility improvement, and pain relief.

Home Laboratory Sampling

Certified phlebotomists visiting your home to safely collect blood, urine, or other clinical samples. Samples are analyzed at our accredited central labs with digital results uploaded to your portal.
